# Class 9 — official section accounting

Ganita Manjari, Grade 9, Parts I and II. Every numbered heading below was located
in the chapter body and checked against the rendered page, not taken from a
detector total.

## Why detector output could not be trusted

The book prints section headings in two forms: small caps, and with a **trailing
full stop** — "4.4. More Identities". A pattern requiring whitespace after the
number silently dropped **§2.4, §3.1, §4.4 and §13.4**. All four were found on
the printed pages. Three different threshold settings produced 50, 52 and 70
sections before the corrected pattern and visual checks settled it at **74**.

## Source anomalies

- **Chapter 9, "Propositions and their Converses", carries no numbered sections
  at all.** Its seven pages run on Statement / Proposition / Discussion /
  Chapter Summary headings with no numbers. Confirmed by rendering every page —
  not inferred from a detector returning nothing. It is a real chapter with a
  real body and zero numbered sections.
- **§4.4 "More Identities"** prints its number with a trailing full stop, the
  same form that cost Class 8 its §3.4. Part I's earlier count of 53 is
  **confirmed**, with §4.4 included.
