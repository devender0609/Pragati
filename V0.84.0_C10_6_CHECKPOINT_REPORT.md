## E. Architecture — general extension

Class 9 drew coordinates as hand-authored `geometry_figure` points with no
equation behind them. `coordinate_plane` exists only as a name in a list.
Authoring line coordinates separately from equations is **exactly the drift
this checkpoint targets**, so `LinearSystemGraphSpec` holds the *system* and
derives every endpoint, the intersection and the stated relationship.

Two deliberate choices in `linearSystem.ts`:

- **Cross products, never ratios.** `a₁/a₂ === b₁/b₂` divides by a
  coefficient that may be zero and compares floats; `a₁b₂ === a₂b₁` does
  neither. A test classifies `x = 3` with `y = 5` — a ratio classifier would
  divide by zero there.
- **Exact rationals.** The fractional system solves to 23/13, 11/13 and is
  verified exactly; a test confirms the rounded pair (1.77, 0.85) **fails**
  the same check.

## G/H. Three states, and graph ↔ algebra

Every system is asserted to map `unique → intersecting`,
`no_solution → distinct_parallel`, `infinitely_many → coincident`. The
validator refuses a pair whose two readings disagree, so the contradiction
is not expressible rather than merely absent.

**Consistent is not unique.** A test asserts the distinction is *stated*, not
just avoided, and that "many solutions" never appears except as "infinitely
many solutions".

Coincidence is established by **scalar multiple** (the factor is 2 here), not
by finding shared points.

## I/J/K/L. Substitution, elimination, and the degenerate readings

Both methods are traced procedures, and tests assert they reach the
classifier's verdict **and the same solution** on all five systems, with
every traced step carrying reasoning over 40 characters.

`0 = 0` and `0 = k` are returned as a `degenerateReading`, and the prose for
both says *"information, not an accident"* — asserted by test. Neither is
reported as a solution.
