# Class 8 → Class 9 — progression audit

Written after all 74 Class 9 section intents and 51 subsection roles were
recorded from the bodies, and compared against the **verified Class 8
decomposition** rather than against chapter titles or memory.

**[S8]** Class 8 source evidence · **[S9]** Class 9 source evidence ·
**[P]** progression interpretation.

## Number systems and irrationality — PROOF_DEEPENING, NEW_NAMED_OBJECT

**[S8]** Class 8 Part II chapter 2 argues that **no terminating decimal squares to
2**, giving the programme its first length that is not a fraction, and proves the
Baudhayana-Pythagoras theorem by area dissection.
**[S9]** §3.5 proves root 2 irrational by **contradiction** from a fraction in
lowest terms (§3.5.1), constructs the length geometrically on the number line
(§3.5.2), and §3.7 closes the line as rationals together with irrationals.
**[P]** The *fact* is not new; the **method of proof and the completed real line
are**. Calling irrationality "introduced in Class 9" would be wrong.

## Exponents and place value — CONTINUATION

**[S8]** Class 8 §2.3 motivates zero and negative exponents by preserving the
division pattern, and Part I chapter 3 generalises place value to an arbitrary
base.
**[S9]** §3.6 explains terminating versus repeating decimals through long-division
remainders, and §3.6.2 explores cyclic numbers.
**[P]** The same place-value machinery, turned on decimal representation.

## Algebraic identities — EXTENSION, not introduction

**[S8]** Class 8 extends the distributive property to products of expressions,
names an identity, and derives the square-of-a-sum and difference-of-squares.
**[S9]** §4.4 adds the three-term square, §4.7 the **cube identities** and the
factorisations of x³ ± y³, §4.5-4.6 move from algebra tiles to coefficient
comparison, and §4.8 applies the identities to **rational expressions**.
**[P]** EXTENSION in object (cubes, three terms) and REPRESENTATIONAL_SHIFT from
tile model to symbolic comparison. The word "identity" is already Class 8's.

## Coordinates — NEW_NAMED_OBJECT

**[S8]** The verified Class 8 decomposition contains no coordinate-plane section.
**[S9]** Chapter 1 builds the 2-D Cartesian system, quadrant sign conventions, and
derives the **distance formula** from the Baudhayana-Pythagoras relation (§1.4).
**[P]** This is the clearest genuinely new named object of the year — and note it
*uses* the Class 8 theorem rather than reproving it.

## Linear relationships — FORMALISATION

**[S8]** Class 8 solves linear equations and works with proportional and inverse
relationships.
**[S9]** §2.6 reads slope and intercept off families of graphs; Part II §13.3
**names** slope as rise over run and derives y = mx + c; §13.5-13.6 solve *pairs*
by substitution, elimination and graph, and classify a pair by coefficient ratios
into one, infinitely many, or no solutions.
**[P]** FORMALISATION of what Class 8 did informally, plus a genuine extension to
simultaneous equations.

## Sequences — FORMALISATION

**[S8]** Class 8 works with patterns and generalises number pyramids and grids
algebraically.
**[S9]** Chapter 8 separates **explicit** from **recursive** rules (§8.2, §8.3),
names arithmetic and geometric progressions, and derives aₙ = a+(n-1)d and the
sum of the first n naturals by pairing (§8.5).
**[P]** The pattern work is continuous; **naming AP and GP and deriving closed
forms is new**. §8.6.1 explicitly recalls the Grade 8 Sierpinski construction and
reads a GP out of it — the book itself marks the continuity.

## Geometry and proof — REASONING_DEEPENING, not introduction

**[S8]** Class 8 proves every quadrilateral property using congruence rather than
measuring, and proves area formulas by dissection.
**[S9]** Chapter 5 proves the chord, perpendicular-bisector and
angle-subtended-by-an-arc theorems, with the semicircle corollary; Part II §12.2
proves the parallelogram properties **and their converses**, §12.3 the Midpoint
Theorem and its converse.
**[P]** Proof is not new. What is new is the **circle** as a proof context and the
systematic pairing of each statement with its converse.

## Chapter 9, propositions and converses — FORMALISATION of logical language

**[S8]** Class 8 contains deductive geometry, parity impossibility arguments and
algebraic reasoning; it reasons, and it refutes.
**[S9]** Chapter 9 names **statement, proposition, converse, counterexample** and
**implication**, writes "If X then Y" as "X implies Y", and shows the
Baudhayana-Pythagoras theorem and its converse are different statements both of
which happen to be true.
**[P]** FORMALISATION. It would be false to say Class 9 introduces reasoning or
proof — the Class 8 evidence contradicts that. What Class 9 introduces is the
**vocabulary and notation for talking about statements themselves**, and the rule
that one counterexample refutes a universal claim. Chapters 5 and 12 then use that
language, which is why this chapter sits where it does.

## Mensuration — EXTENSION

**[S8]** Class 8 Part II chapter 7 derives area formulas for rectangles,
triangles, parallelograms, rhombuses and trapeziums by dissection.
**[S9]** Chapter 6 adds the **circle**: the C/D ratio as a constant, pi's
irrationality (§6.3, resting on §3.5), arc length, sector area and the area of a
disc by Archimedes' sector argument; plus **Heron's** and **Brahmagupta's**
formulas, with Heron shown as the special case. Part II chapter 14 moves to three
dimensions — cylinder, cone, sphere, hemisphere.
**[P]** EXTENSION of method (dissection and limiting arguments) to curved figures
and to solids.

## Data — EXTENSION

**[S8]** Class 8 proves how the mean behaves as a balance point and under shifts.
**[S9]** Part II §10.1 generalises to the **weighted** mean, motivated by the error
of averaging averages; §10.2 adds stacked and 100% stacked displays and asks what
each can and cannot show.
**[P]** EXTENSION, with a new critical demand about display choice.

## Probability — NEW_NAMED_OBJECT

**[S9]** Chapter 7 defines randomness, the 0-1 scale, experimental against
theoretical probability, sample space and event, and tree diagrams; it names the
**gambler's fallacy** as a misconception to refute.
**[P]** No counterpart appears in the verified Class 8 decomposition, so this is
recorded as new for the programme — stated against that evidence, not from
assumption.

## Algorithms — NEW_NAMED_OBJECT with an old substrate

**[S8]** Class 8 proves divisibility tests algebraically from place value.
**[S9]** Part II chapter 11 treats a **method itself** as the object: column
addition as an algorithm, three successive gcd algorithms, and §11.3.2 **compares
their effort** rather than their answers.
**[P]** Efficiency as a property of a procedure is new. Note this is
Computational Thinking material appearing inside the Mathematics textbook; it
remains cross-cutting and is not a Mathematics reporting domain.

## What is NOT claimed

No claim that Class 9 introduces proof, reasoning, irrational quantities,
identities or patterns. In each case the verified Class 8 decomposition already
contains the idea, and the Class 9 move is formalisation, extension or a change
of representation.
