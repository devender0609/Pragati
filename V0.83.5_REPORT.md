## Product truth

**1. What Step 2 said before**

> **Assign an assessment** — Pick a skill (or a mixed mode), set the size,
> and tell the class what to take next.
> **Students complete it** — Students see 10 short items adapted to how
> they answer. No timer.

"Adapted to how they answer" describes a calibrated adaptive test. Growth
is frozen, the picker is a rule of thumb, and Assign was reframed around
learning at an earlier release. v0.83.4 fixed Step 1 and left this
standing, one screen later.

**2. What it says now**

> 1. **Create a classroom** — Add a small roster. Classrooms sync to the
>    cloud when you sign in.
> 2. **Assign learning or practice** — Choose a chapter or a skill from
>    the class's own textbook and set what the class should work on next.
> 3. **Students learn and practise** — Students work through a short set
>    of questions at their own pace. No timer, and a wrong answer
>    explains the mistake behind it.
> 4. **You review the work and decide what is next** — The class
>    dashboard surfaces the hardest skills, the most common mistakes, and
>    anything flagged for you to read. What to teach next is your call,
>    not the software's.

Steps 3 and 4 (pilot choice, quick setup) were audited and carry no
product claim; they are unchanged.

**3. What `index.html` said before**

> Pragati: prototype adaptive growth assessment for CBSE Class 6 Math
> (FR.06).

That is the sentence search results and link previews quote. It claimed
Growth, claimed adaptivity and scoped the product to Class 6.

**4. What it says now**

> Pragati is a mathematics learning and practice platform aligned to
> current CBSE/NCERT Mathematics courses.

`<title>` is "Pragati — Mathematics Learning" (corrected at v0.83.2).
There are no OpenGraph, Twitter or manifest metadata blocks in the
document; the two tags above are the whole of the product metadata.

sweep is `ACTIVE_PRODUCT_CLAIM_AUDIT.md` — 826 readable occurrences of
