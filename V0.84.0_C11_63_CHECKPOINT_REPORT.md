## What the bundle says, and does not

> "Automated checks establish that a lesson is **well-formed**. They cannot
> establish that it **teaches**."

It states plainly that a reviewer is free to disagree, and that a lesson
the automation called ready can come back MISALIGNED. A test asserts the
bundle never says *already aligned*, *pre-reviewed* or *approved*.

## Version locking and emptiness

Each packet's instructional fingerprint is printed for the reviewer to
copy. Mismatch yields `STALE_REVIEW_RESPONSE` and is not applied.

Four empty response templates, every judgement `null`. A test feeds an
untouched template to the validator and asserts it is **rejected** — an
unfilled form is not a review.

The §7.2 template carries four unit slots, and a test confirms a split
verdict validates: ALIGNED · ALIGNED · PARTIAL · MISALIGNED with REVISE
and SHOULD_SPLIT. That is the result this pilot exists to be able to
capture.
