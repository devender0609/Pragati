## D/E. Visual inspection — the gate that fails

| | |
|---|---|
| Total PDF pages | 368 |
| Chapter instructional pages | 313 |
| Pages **directly inspected visually** | **~75** |
| `visualRequired` (rule-classified) | **105** |
| `visualRequired` directly inspected | **49** |

The automated candidate set was 166. Looking at two contact sheets covering
56 pages showed two false-positive classes the brief had warned about:
**chapter opener pages** (a portrait of a mathematician plus a QR code) and
**pages whose only "drawing" is a tinted text box** around a summary or
historical note. Neither is mathematical visual evidence.

I rebuilt the rule on stroke-versus-fill geometry, excluded openers, and
tuned it against the 49 pages I had actually seen. **No threshold achieves
both full recall and full precision.** At the chosen setting the rule scores
**32/36 true positives and 13/13 true negatives** — it never calls decorative
material mathematical, but it misses four figures that carry no `Fig`
caption and little stroke area.

So **105 is a validated lower bound, not a count**, and
`visualRequiredInspected (49) ≠ visualRequired (105)`. The gate fails
honestly. The deeper finding is that tinted boxes and small figures overlap
in every measurable feature, so this classification cannot be automated to
completion — the remaining pages need looking at.

diagrams and function graphs (2), trigonometric diagrams and function graphs
