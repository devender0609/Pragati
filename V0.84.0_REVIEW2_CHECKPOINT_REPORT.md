## B. Methodology, and how it failed twice first

Three passes. The first two are worth reporting because they were wrong.

**Pass 1** flagged **31 blockers**. I inspected all of them against the source
text before recording a single one. **Every one was a false positive** — the
harness matched fragments of longer expressions. `4 + 5 + 6 = 15` was read as
`5 + 6 = 15`; `21 × 8 × 7 × 6 = 7056` as `7 × 6 = 7056`; `−15 + 15 = 0` as
`15 + 15 = 0`. The mathematics was correct in all 31.

**Pass 2** evaluated complete expressions instead and flagged **2**. Both were
also false: `4/10 = 2/5` read as `= 2`, and a chained equality
`30 × 12 + 20 × 22 = 360 + 440 = 800` matched against its first intermediate.

**Pass 3**, with terminal-value matching, flagged **0**.

The discipline that matters here is that a flagged finding is a hypothesis,
not a defect. Reporting 31 blockers would have been worse than reporting none.

**Independence.** I did not reason "checkpoint 39 passed probability, so
probability is correct." The chapters were read fresh and the arithmetic
recomputed from the lesson text, not from prior audit output.
