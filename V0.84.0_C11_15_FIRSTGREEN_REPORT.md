## The architecture

One semantic object per conic drives its locus, parameters, standard
equation, focal data, eccentricity, latus rectum and drawn figure. Each is a
discriminated union carrying only the parameters its geometry has.

Every invariant carries a negative fixture, so the check tests the geometry
rather than reproducing its own expectation:

| Invariant | Holds | Fails off the curve |
|---|---|---|
| Focus-directrix | (2,4) and (8,8) on `y² = 8x` | (2,5) and (1,4) |
| Focal sum = 2a | vertex and (0,3) give 10 | (1,1) does not |
| Focal difference = 2a | vertex gives 6 | (1,1) does not |

Exact throughout: ellipse `e = 4/5`, hyperbola `e = 5/3`, latus rectum
`18/5` and `32/3`. `c² = a² − b²` against `c² = a² + b²` puts ellipse foci
inside the vertices and hyperbola foci beyond them.

Both ellipse orientations are distinct objects producing distinct figures —
`x²/25 + y²/9 = 1` against `x²/9 + y²/25 = 1`. The hyperbola draws as **two
separate polylines** with opposite signs.

Contradictory objects throw: zero radius, `b > a`, equal semi-axes.
