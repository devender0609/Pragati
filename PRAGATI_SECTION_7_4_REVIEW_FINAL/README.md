# Please review one Class 6 Fractions lesson

**Review candidate: `S74-v1-A1A3FF`**
**Build: `pragati-v0.66.0-review-candidate`**

Thank you for agreeing to look at this. It should take about an hour.

## What we are asking

We have built **one** lesson — Class 6 Fractions, part 7.4, "Marking
Fraction Lengths on the Number Line" (*Ganita Prakash*, page 160).

We want to know whether it is good enough to put in front of children.
We have built only this one on purpose: if the approach is wrong, we
would rather find out after one lesson than after fifty.

**Please say so if it is not good enough.** That is a useful answer, and
it is why we are asking before building more.

## How to see the lesson

The copy of Pragati sent with this pack is **fixed**. It cannot change
while you review it, so what you see is what your answers will apply to.

1. Unzip `pragati-v0.66.0.zip`.
2. In that folder run:
   ```
   npm ci
   npm run build
   npm run preview
   ```
3. Open the address it prints (usually `http://localhost:4173`).
4. Switch to **Teacher mode** (top right).
5. Choose **Admin & Research** → **Curriculum coverage**.
6. Choose **"Open the Section 7.4 reviewer preview"**.

**Please try the practice questions yourself** — tap the number line,
type a fraction, choose a strip. They work, and several of our questions
are about how they behave.

Please look at it on a phone as well as a computer if you can. One of
our questions is specifically about whether the diagrams are readable on
a small screen.

## How to answer

Open `PACKAGE_B_FOR_REVIEWER.md`, fill in the two right-hand columns for
each question, and send it back. For each choose one of:

**Accept** · **Revise** · **Reject** · **Insufficient evidence**

and write a reason. The reason matters more than the rating — if you
choose Revise, tell us what to change.

You do not need to open or edit anything else.

## Three things worth knowing

**This lesson is not live.** No child can reach it, and it will stay
that way until this review is complete. Passing review does not publish
it either — that takes a separate, deliberate step.

**This review is about quality, not proof of learning.** We are asking
whether the mathematics is right, whether it matches the textbook,
whether it suits 11-year-olds, and whether it is usable. We are *not*
claiming — and this review does not establish — that it improves
results. That would need a different kind of study.

**Your answers apply to this version only.** The code `S74-v1-A1A3FF`
identifies the exact lesson and build you were sent. If we change the
content afterwards, our system refuses to treat this review as covering
the new version, and we will come back to you. Your approval cannot be
quietly reused for something you did not see.

## What else is being reviewed separately

A second, shorter pack asks a curriculum specialist three questions
about whether this lesson is mapped to the right part of the curriculum.
That is a different kind of expertise and we are not asking you to
answer it. **Both** reviews are needed before this lesson could ever be
shown to a child.
