## Coverage

**73 of 74 sections are covered by units.** §3.7 "Conclusion: The Never-Ending
Journey" carries a **REVIEW** disposition: it restates the completed number line
and holds consolidated exercises belonging to the sections that taught them. Its
body is still accounted for, by a source segment, so no page falls out of the
model.

Chapter 9 prints no numbered section. Its two units are built on

## Where sections split

The largest splits follow a change in reasoning demand rather than length.
§1.4 splits because deriving the distance formula and applying it to collinearity
are different demands. §3.5 splits the proof by contradiction from the geometric
construction. §5.7 splits the doubling theorem from the same-segment deduction.
§7.2 splits experimental from theoretical probability, then gives the comparison —
and the gambler's fallacy — its own unit. §11.3 separates *judging* two algorithms
from Euclid's method, because comparing effort is the chapter's real demand.

## Where sections merge

§1.1+§1.2, §2.1+§2.2, §5.1+§5.2 and §6.6+§6.7 each merge because one section
exists only to set up the next: §6.6 is a single page supplying the rectangle area
that §6.7 immediately uses. None was merged to reduce the count.

## Subsection evidence in use

The 46 subsections marked as materially changing demand drove most splits — §3.5.1
(the contradiction proof), §7.2.1/§7.2.2 (experimental versus theoretical),
§11.3.2 (algorithm analysis), §12.3.1/§12.3.2 (Midpoint Theorem and its
consequences), §14.5.2 (hemisphere, where curved-versus-total is the error point).
They were read as evidence, not applied as a rule: §3.1.1 and §3.1.2 are both
historical and stay inside one unit.
