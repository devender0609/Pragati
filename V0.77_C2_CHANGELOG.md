## 1. Semantic defects corrected

These were the two notes that mattered most, and both were real.

**C1's biggest panel was a false claim.** It was headed "Open now" and
listed 7.2, 7.5, 7.6 and 7.8 by their official Ganita Prakash section
numbers and titles — a statement that Pragati teaches four sections of
the current textbook. It does not. Nine official Fractions sections are
authored as drafts, none reviewed, none published; what a student can
actually open is older Pragati practice authored against a superseded
book.

The product already knew this. The shipped chapter landing has said
"Related practice — not a chapter lesson" since v0.70, and there is a
canonical helper, `relatedPracticeForChapter`, that returns plain
student-facing names. My concept reached past that policy to the raw
section list because the raw section list made a better-looking panel.
That is the exact pressure §18 warns about, and it took one screen to
appear.

C2 calls the canonical helper. It returns **four** activities from nine
sections, because three §7.8 skills share one name — a de-duplication I
would have got wrong by hand. The official sections are now named
separately: *"All 9 sections of this chapter are written and waiting on
teacher review."*

**"Ready to learn" is gone from the shelf.** Fractions now reads
"Practice available", derived from provenance rather than asserted.

> **A production defect this surfaced.** The shipped
> `studentAvailabilityLabel` returns "Ready to learn" for Fractions
> today, because it counts legacy skill lessons as instruction. That is
> wrong in the product, not only in my concept, and it should be fixed
> in whatever change adopts this direction. I have not touched it,
> because you asked me not to touch production.

**"Continue learning" is gone.** The CTA is derived: an official lesson
gives "Start Fractions", related practice only gives "Explore
Fractions", nothing eligible gives "Explore your curriculum". A
first-run student is never offered a continuation.

Preserved unchanged: unavailable chapters non-interactive, official vs
legacy provenance, review and publication gates, no fake progress,
§7.4 fingerprint, Growth lock. No product file was modified.

## 2. Composition changes

- **The white card is gone (§1).** The primary surface is a dark studio
  panel carrying the same lattice as the canvas, overlapping it.
- **The panels are no longer equal (§2).** Primary is 1.74× the width of
  secondary and roughly twice its height; the teal panel is only as tall
  as its content; the record is **one line of type with no panel at
  all** (§10). Page height at 1440 fell from 2,349px to 1,818px.
- **Hero copy rewritten (§6)** to describe the chapter rather than one
  figure on it.
- **The empty lower right now works (§7):** the notation is set at 74px
  with a saffron rule, and a dashed connector runs from the boundary of
  the shaded strip down to the point on the line — so "these are the
  same number" is drawn, not asserted.
- **The hero does mathematics (§8).** Five choices re-partition the
  strip, walk the point and rewrite the notation together, with CSS
  transitions on geometry so `prefers-reduced-motion` removes the
  movement and the figure still lands correctly.
  `c2-interaction-1440.png` shows the 2/3 state against 3/5.
- **Mobile shelf snaps horizontally (§9).** Same markup, same ten
  chapters, same official order — laid across instead of down. Page
  height at 390 fell from 3,930px to 2,386px.
- **A's typographic discipline (§12):** chapter numeral as display type
  rather than a chip, 68px title, hairlines instead of containers inside
  the primary panel.

One thing I caught in my own first C2 capture: the practice rows reused
`ChapterArtwork` at 32px, which crushed a three-representation drawing
into an unreadable smudge — the "tiny icon" failure reintroduced by me
at small size. They are now four glyphs drawn *for* 24px, each saying
which activity it is rather than saying "fractions" four times.

## 3. What still feels weak

- **The teal panel leaves a gap under it at 1440.** Sizing it to content
  was right; the column it sits in is now short against a tall primary.
- **The canvas is cramped at 390.** The notation squeezes against the
  right edge. It should probably drop below the figure on narrow
  widths rather than share the row.
- **Nine tiles reading "Being written"** is honest and still repetitive.
  B's station treatment is the likeliest fix, and I have kept B for that
  reason.
- **This is still the first-run state.** Every capture shows a student
  with no history, because that is the only honest state for a fresh
  profile. I have not seen this composition with real activity in it.
- **The interaction is five buttons, not a manipulable object.** It
  proves the studio idea; it is not the thing §8 eventually wants.
