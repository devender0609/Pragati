## What changed

**§1 mobile hero.** The canvas now has two layouts. `wide` keeps strip,
line and notation in one row; `stacked` gives the diagram the full width
and drops the notation onto its own row beneath, still at 78px. Proved
at 360, 390 and 430 — page heights 2578 / 2550 / 2517, no overflow.

**§2 student copy.** "waiting on teacher review" is a description of our
approval workflow, and a Class 6 student is not its audience. Now:
*"Chapter lessons are being prepared. Practice is available now."*

**§3 shelf repetition.** Nine tiles no longer repeat a status. It is
said once, in the shelf lede: *"The ones drawn in outline are coming
later."* The underlying status is unchanged, the tiles stay muted, and
they remain non-interactive.

**§4 returning state.** Seeded deterministically in one place in
`main.tsx`, not sprinkled through components: one unfinished set and two
completed activities, with everything on screen derived from those by
the same rules the first-run state uses. The CTA becomes "Continue
practice" and names what is unfinished; completed activities show
"Practised"; the record line carries real counts. No mastery, no
percentage, no streak — Pragati does not compute those.

CTA ladder, all four verified: first run → *Explore Fractions*;
unfinished → *Continue practice*; official lesson → *Start Fractions*;
nothing eligible → *Explore your curriculum*. No-history never says
Continue.

**§6-§9 Learn.** Three registers instead of one grid: the open chapter
as a studio with its artwork at scale and its real activities inside;
a station rail carrying all ten chapters in book order; then the
remaining nine as large plates. Per §9 this takes B's **node** and not
its path — one straight rule, no serpentine, and **no filled segment**,
so the rail says "the book is in this order" and never "you have come
this far".

**§10-§16 the lesson.** Real content: every word comes from
`lessonFor('FR.02')` and `ITEMS`. Five stages, one dominating, rail
always reachable — a set of buttons, not a next-only stepper, so it is
not a wizard that traps the student. Stage heights at 1440 are
973-1041px; the v0.76 lesson was one 4,500px page.

Two framings are mine and are marked in the code: the stage names (which
the shipped lesson already used) and the Think deeper prompt, which is
built **from** the authored common mistake rather than invented beside
it. No mathematical claim was rewritten. FR.02 is legacy skill content,
not an official §7.x lesson, and the header says so.

Try it: all four options are drawn identically until the student
commits, so nothing about the layout leaks the answer. A wrong choice
gets the authored `fix` text, not "try again".
