### Three defects, all in my own extraction

**Body descent, first attempt.** The regex matched any decimal numeral, so it
reported 142 sections — including §10.33 in a chapter that has nothing close
to that many. Replaced.

**Body descent, second attempt.** Requiring a heading-like capitalised phrase
cut it to 99, still inflated: it was matching numerals followed by the
**"Reprint 2026-27" page footer**, figure captions and table data. Unresolved.

**Chapter titles.** The regex assumed "Chapter N" on its own line, which holds
in Chapter 1 and almost nowhere else. 11 of 14 titles missing.

### One genuine finding

**§7.3 "Pascal's triangle" was found by body descent and missed by the
heading scan** — a wrapped heading invisible to a line-start scan. This is
the exact Class 10 failure mode the brief warned about, now confirmed to
occur in Class 11 as well. It is also the reason the heading-scan 62 must be
treated as a floor: if one section wrapped, others may have.
