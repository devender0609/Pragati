## C. Representation audit

Class 9 Chapter 14 drew **no solids**. It used flat nets, deliberately,
because a net states the mathematics and a perspective drawing only suggests
it. `GeometryFigureSpec` holds points, segments and angles — it can draw a
shape but cannot say anything about surfaces.

So the gap was not drawing. **Nothing could express that a surface still
exists while no longer being on the outside.** That distinction is the entire
difficulty of the chapter and is invisible in any picture, because an internal
surface is by definition the one you cannot see.

## D/E. The extension — architecture verdict **B**

`CompositeSolidSpec`, a general capability that knows nothing about Chapter 12:

- **Components** declare a kind and dimensions. Their surfaces are **derived**
  from the kind — an author cannot forget one or invent one.
- **Joins** name two surfaces. The exposed set is then **derived**, never
  authored, so prose and arithmetic read the same object.
- `exposedSurfaceArea`, `compositeVolume`, and `naiveTotalSurfaceArea` — the
  last kept so a lesson can *show* the wrong answer rather than warn about it.

Deliberately not built: boolean geometry, meshes, cavities, any 3-D engine.
The figure draws a labelled **cross-section**, following the Class 9 judgement.
