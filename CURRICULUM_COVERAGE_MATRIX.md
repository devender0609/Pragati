## The five states a section can be in

v0.74 §5 established these, and they stay stated separately because
collapsing any two of them is how a draft starts looking like a lesson.

1. **No instructional content at all** — the official record exists in
   Pragati and nothing has been written for it.
2. **Partial draft** — some of Learn, practice, reasoning or teacher
   notes exists, but not enough to be reviewable.
3. **Complete draft, no review package** — reviewable in substance, but
   an educator cannot yet read it because the package is unbuilt. This
   is engineering work.
4. **Complete draft, review-ready** — blocked on a person, not on us.
5. **Reviewed and published** — the only state a student may see.

Currently: **9** sections are in state 4, **0** in state 3, and
**0** in state 5.
