## The defect §5 was written to catch

`AssessmentAssignment` has carried this comment since v0.11: the title
is *"surfaced on the student's assignment card"*, and setting
`active: false` means *"the student home no longer surfaces it"*.

**No such card existed anywhere in the product.** A teacher could create
an assignment, see it listed under Assign, and no student would ever be
shown it.

It survived every release because every test exercised the teacher form
and the storage layer, and both worked perfectly. The hop nobody tested
was the last one. That is precisely why §5 asks for end-to-end
verification rather than form-level, and I would not have found it by
looking at either screen.

**Student change, and why it qualifies.** The lock permits a change for
a functional defect. This is one: the fix restores a capability the
product claims in its own type definition. Student Home now shows "Your
teacher set this", the teacher's title and note, and a control that
opens *that* assignment. Four tests pin it, including that nothing shows
when nothing is assigned and that an assignment cannot impersonate a
Growth Check. No locked composition changed beyond adding the band the
claim requires.

A second routing defect turned up beside it: Overview's own "Open
Classes" and "Create assignment" buttons still jumped to the standalone
top-level routes, so a teacher pressing them lost the rail and the class
scope — even after the tab switcher was fixed. Both now switch tabs.

## The six destinations

**Overview** — recomposed. Empty state whose largest elements are its
three actions, plus two panels of real fact. Fixed a count computed from
`availability === 'available'`, which was the "Ready to learn" defect in
teacher language: a chapter whose only openable content is legacy
practice counted as a chapter with a lesson.

**Classes** — header and empty roster recomposed. "Growth history"
removed; Pragati records sessions and does not measure growth.

**Assign** — grouped by availability rather than four equal rows:
Available now, Not yet available, A separate governed product. Desktop
is a work column plus a context rail. I got the composition wrong once
in between: the first two-column attempt left three zeros alone in the
left column, the same "three statistics and a void" shape inside a grid.

**Assess** — the two tracks named before either is offered.
Instructional check is classroom evidence a teacher interprets; Growth
carries calibration and norming claims and is never derived from a
check.

**Insights** — real header, purposeful empty state, and a panel saying
what Insights will **not** show: no mastery or ability score, no
class-level growth, no diagnosis from a single wrong answer. That is the
assumption a teacher is likeliest to make wrongly.

**Resources** — the biggest change, found only by looking. A teacher met
a twelve-row matrix of UNKNOWN cells and columns headed "In Pragati /
Reviewed / Complete drafts" before reaching anything teachable. v0.62
and v0.68 had moved that content into Teacher for good reasons and the
destination was wrong. It is behind one link to Admin & Research; a
depth panel states what each grade's evidence supports.

**Navigation copy** — Insights read "Class-level growth, weak concepts,
and misconceptions". Pragati computes none of those. All five rewritten,
with a test failing on any such phrase.

**Mobile** — all six reachable at 360/390/430, asserted by a harness
that now works. It was missing a click: the header toggle names the
*current* mode, so switching it changes the chrome without navigating.

## Curriculum integrity

Book identity is recorded separately from structure. Classes 1–5, 7, 8
carry `secondary_discovery_only` titles — agreeing secondary sources are
not evidence, as v0.51 demonstrated. Class 6 no longer reports an
invented unit layer: Units —, Chapters 10, Sections 65. Classes 10–12
cannot acquire a chapter count from topic titles.

## Honest weaknesses

- **Every capture is an empty state.** No populated-state fixtures
  exist, so no Teacher screen has been reviewed with real class data in
  it. Insights and Classes are the two whose composition would change
  most.
- **Classes and Assess** are thin at 1440 — correct and calm, but they
  are a header plus one or two panels.
- Assign's left column is still mostly empty when nothing is assigned,
  which is the honest state but not a composed one.

## Is Teacher ready to lock?

**Not yet.** It is coherent, truthful and functional, and every route
works on every width. Lock should wait for populated-state review — a
professional product judged only in its empty state has not been judged.
