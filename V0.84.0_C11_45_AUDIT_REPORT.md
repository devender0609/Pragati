## Finding 1 — the nine-unit debt, reframed

`GD-1` has been carried since checkpoint 94 as *nine units whose
optional-enrichment status contradicts the policy*. The audit changes what
that means:

**Six of the nine are already authored and shipped.**

| Authored | Not authored |
|---|---|
| RF-FO2, TRG-FO3, CPX-FO5a, CPX-FO5b, BIN-FO7, STL-FO9 | SEQ-FO8, 3DG-FO10, LIM-FO11 |

So the debt is mostly a **status-label question, not an authoring gap**.

need authoring at all.

That is a materially smaller and differently-shaped problem than "nine
units pending".

## Finding 2 — new: CBSE-only units given chapter ownership

Three units are `CBSE_ONLY` with `ncertChapterOwner: null` in the
decomposition, yet their authored artifacts declare an NCERT chapter owner:

| Unit | Artifact | Artifact owner |
|---|---|---|
| C11-RF-FO2 | `ncert_kemh1_fo2_composition_lesson` | `ncert_kemh1_ch02` |
| C11-TRG-FO3 | `ncert_kemh1_fo3_general_solutions_lesson` | `ncert_kemh1_ch03` |
| C11-CPX-FO5b | `ncert_kemh1_fo5b_complex_quadratics_lesson` | `ncert_kemh1_ch04` |

**This is the exact error C11-INQ-FO6 was protected from** at checkpoints
94 and 96C — a CBSE-only topic carrying a bibliographic chapter it does not
belong to. These three predate that policy and were never revisited.

Not affected: `SF-1-FORMATIVE`, `SF-2-FORMATIVE`, `SF-3-FORMATIVE` and
`FO-1-APPLICATION`. Those are **scope fragments of real NCERT sections**, so
chapter ownership is correct for them. The distinction is the whole point:
a fragment of §4.5 belongs to Chapter 4; a CBSE-only topic with no NCERT
section belongs to no chapter.

Recorded as `GD-2-CBSE-ONLY-CHAPTER-OWNERSHIP`. **Not repaired here.**
Repairing it would move up to three lessons out of chapter-owned into
chapter-less, changing `85 / 1 / 1 / 87` and three chapter row counts.
