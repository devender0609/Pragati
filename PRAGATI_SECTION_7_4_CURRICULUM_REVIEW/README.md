# Three curriculum questions about one Class 6 lesson

**Snapshot: Package A, §7.4, version 1**

Thank you for looking at this. It should take **20–30 minutes**.

## What we are asking

We have written one lesson for Class 6 Fractions and want to know
whether we have placed it correctly in the curriculum. **Three
questions only.** We are not asking you to review the teaching quality —
a practising Class 6 teacher is doing that separately.

## What this review establishes

That the lesson is mapped to the right part of the curriculum and the
right competencies. It does **not** establish that the teaching is good
(a separate review) or that it improves learning (neither review does).

## Your answers apply to this mapping only

If we change the mapping afterwards, we will ask again rather than treat
your answers as covering the new one.
