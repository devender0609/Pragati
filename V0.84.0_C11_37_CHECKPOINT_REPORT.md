## F. Instructional-status policy, stated explicitly

- **REQUIRED_CORE** — inside the official instructional scope, whether or
  not it is summatively assessed
- **REQUIRED_PREREQUISITE** — required because downstream required content
  depends on it
- **OPTIONAL_ENRICHMENT** — available, but **outside** the official scope
  and not needed by downstream required content

### H. The key policy test

> May Pragati classify official syllabus content as instructionally optional?

**No.** The CBSE preamble says these topics are *"included in the syllabus
but will be assessed only formatively"*. Included in the syllabus places a
topic inside the official instructional scope, which is exactly what
`instructionalStatus` records. That a topic is never summatively assessed
is recorded separately, by `assessmentStatus`. Collapsing the two is what
makes formative read as optional.

## K/L. The STL-01 prerequisite: removed

`C11-STL-01` is **"Slope of a line"** — *compute the slope of a line from
two points or from its angle of inclination*.

FO-6 needs a coordinate plane, a boundary line, and a test point to decide
which side satisfies the inequality. **None of that uses slope.** All of it
is available before Class 11. The edge was ordering convenience, not
dependency, and keeping it would have forced FO-6 to wait for Chapter 10.

**Final prerequisites: `[C11-INQ-02]`.**
