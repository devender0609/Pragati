## The status-model correction

Checkpoint 9 marked 33 Class 3 units `NEEDS_HUMAN_CHECK` when 29 of them were
simply waiting for pages to be rendered. That turned missing evidence into a
pretend review queue and would have sent an author looking for a decision
nobody had to make. Three states now, derived by one function:

| State | Means |
|---|---|
| `READY_FOR_AUTHORING` | evidence complete, no open decision |
| `NEEDS_HUMAN_CHECK` | evidence complete **and** a named curriculum question |
| `DRAFT_DECOMPOSITION` | required source evidence still incomplete |

A test asserts every unit's stored status equals `derivedStatusFor(u)`, that no
evidence-incomplete unit is ever in the review queue, and that every review
unit carries a question of real length ending in a question mark.

Two related fixes: a chapter whose whole text has been read is never reported
`INDEXED_ONLY` again (it is `PARTIALLY_INSPECTED` until its visuals are done),
and the ambiguous `pagesUnresolved` is replaced by `pagesFullTextPending`,
`visualPagesPending` and `pagesEvidenceIncomplete`, reported separately.
