## The six checkpoint-14 defects, fixed

The Class 6 audit generator was a clone of the primary-grade one: it called the
book **Maths Mela**, claimed **14 chapters and no numbered sections**, and
printed **"0/65 chapters"** because the section denominator had been put in
`chaptersTotal`. All three are corrected, with tests that forbid each false
string. The generator's header comments now state the invariants it enforces.

`chaptersTotal` counts chapters again. The two layers have their own fields:
`officialChapterCount`, `officialSectionsTotal`,
`chaptersFullyInspected / PartiallyInspected / IndexedOnly`, and
`sectionsAccountedFor / NotYetInspected`.

**Fractions is now actually evidence-complete.** Pages 36-50 were read and
rendered. Reading them changed the model: **page 36 is the chapter Summary, not
solutions**, so the segment was split — p36 SUMMARY, pp37-50 REFERENCE. The
chapter derives FULLY_INSPECTED from its evidence, and the test derives that
state rather than asserting it. **The nine Fractions mappings were revalidated
against the completed extent and none changed**: the summary restates the
sections and the answer key answers them; neither teaches an objective the
units lack.
