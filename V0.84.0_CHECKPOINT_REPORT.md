## The four integrity defects, and what was done

1. **Reversed unit counts.** The canonical data said Class 1 = 44 and
   Class 2 = 46; the report said the reverse. Report figures are now
   generated, with a consistency test.
2. **Stale defects document.** It still claimed the five chapter tails
   were unread and that both Puzzles sections introduced nothing new —
   both untrue after checkpoint 2. Rewritten from the dataset, with the
   superseded statements kept under a historical heading.
3. **`FULL_PAGE_INSPECTED` overstated the evidence.** A four-page unit
   could carry the label with one page rendered. Evidence is now a
   per-page ledger — full text read? visual needed? visual done? — and
   the depth label is computed from it.
4. **A part-read chapter could count as inspected.** An official record
   is FULLY_INSPECTED only when its whole extent is; partial progress is
   reported as PARTIALLY_INSPECTED rather than rounded up.

## What reading the whole of Class 1 found

- **Capacity was missing.** Chapter 7 spends three pages filling a bucket
  with jugs, glasses and bowls and comparing containers. Length and
  weight had units; capacity had none. It is now `..._ch07_u4`.
- **The Puzzles section has three roles, not one.** Rehearsal pages
  (REVIEW); constraint reasoning — one shape per row and column, equal
  sums, sum-to-17 (`..._ch13_u3`); and puzzles needing ideas Class 1 has
  never met — "how many times can you subtract 5 from 25", a fruit
  symbol standing for a value, fewest coins for `49 (`..._ch13_u4`,
  REASONING_EXTENSION, flagged for a human scope decision).
- Class 1 now has 47 units against 13 official chapters.
