## The Class 8 audit no longer contradicts itself

Checkpoint 23's page audit said "14 official chapters and **58** numbered
sections" in its headline and "**59/59** numbered sections accounted for" twelve
lines later. The headline was typed into the generator; the body was computed.

Both are derived now — sections from the master map's own Class 8 section
records, chapters from the record extents in scope — and the generator **throws**
if the section count disagrees with `classProgress`. Nothing was hard-coded to
59 either: a future source correction will flow through without prose drift.

A test now requires **every** "N numbered sections" claim in the document to
equal the derived total, so the two halves of the report cannot diverge again.

### The weak invariant is gone

The old test asserted `authoringUnits(8).length >= 58`, which would still have
passed if the duplicated second §2.5 disappeared — the exact regression worth
catching. It now asserts **set equality of the same 59 section ids** across
authoring records, `OfficialSectionExtents`, section accounting and
`classProgress`, and the stale name ("verifies 14 chapters and 58 numbered
sections") is replaced.

both exist, both sit in `ncert_hegp1_ch02`, both are VERIFIED_FROM_SOURCE, their
bodies abut rather than overlap, both are inside the 59, and **no `s2_6` is
invented** — the book prints the number 2.5 twice and prints no 2.6.
