## The four issues, resolved

**1. Two segments still said their pages were unread.** Their
`sourceEvidence.establishes` read "Pages not yet read" while the evidence was
complete — the previous test read only `justification`, so the contradiction
sat one field away. Both are rewritten from the pages: Class 2 Chapter 7
page 79 now records two same-sized bags compared by weight and the kinds of
balance people use; Chapter 11 page 16 records a heading of "Notes" and ruled
space. The other five segments were rewritten the same way, since all had the
generic text. The test now reads `justification`, `establishes` and
`sourceLabel`, for segments, units and non-instructional records alike.

**2. Two labels described ranges the segments no longer span** — "pages 9-12"
on a one-page segment. Labels are now derived from the pdf range at write
time, and `sourceLabelIsFromBook` marks the one label that is a real textbook
heading ("Puzzles") so it is never rewritten. A test compares each generated
label with its evidence.

**3. Chapter 13's rehearsal pages were a whole-record non-instructional
entry** inside a chapter that has four instructional units — which made an
official record that plainly teaches look non-instructional. Migrated to
`pragati_srcseg_g01_ch13_rehearsal`, keeping the range, page evidence, role
and dates. `NonInstructionalRecord` now means what its name says: a complete
official record inspected and found to produce no teaching target. A gate
enforces it, and `recordIsCovered()` no longer accepts a subrange as
whole-record coverage. **The `nonInstructional` array is now empty** — correct,
because every official chapter of Classes 1 and 2 teaches something.

**4. The overlap audit checked only unit-to-unit.** `overlapAudit()` now
covers units, source segments and non-instructional records in every
combination within a record. It finds **20 overlapping pairs, all
INTENTIONAL**, each with a written reason in the data — listed in
`SOURCE_COVERAGE_OVERLAP_AUDIT_CLASSES_1_2.md`. The two unit-to-segment
overlaps now explain themselves specifically: rehearsal and constraint tasks
share a Class 1 spread, and the "Puzzles" heading shares a page with the first
tasks under it.
