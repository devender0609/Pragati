## The record-model correction

Class 6 units stored the **chapter** id in `officialRecordId` while the type
said the numbered section. The consequence was not cosmetic: the
official-record helpers and the master map never saw 53 of the 65 sections, and
`classProgress` typed 65/65 anyway. Two authorities, one of them wrong.

- Every Class 6 unit's `officialRecordId` is now its **numbered section**; the
  chapter lives in its own `officialChapterId`.
- `sectionInspectionState()` and `sectionAccounting()` derive section state
  from the same evidence as everything else; **`officialRecordAccounting(6)`
  now reports 65 total and 65 fully inspected** instead of being waived.
- `instructionalUnitsFor()` still finds a chapter's units, because the chapter
  owns the page extent; `overlapAudit()` groups by chapter, since page overlap
  is a chapter-level question.
- The master map shows **65/65 Class 6 sections `page_level_inspected`** (was
  12/65) and 10/10 chapters. A test fails if a source-complete class has any
  `not_inspected` section.
- `CLASS_6_SECTION_ACCOUNTING.md` lists all 65 sections with their units and
  derived state, so "65/65" is auditable rather than typed.

**officialRecordId violations: 0.**

area model, and the multiply-top-and-bottom rule with its justification. That
