## G. The model extension — general, not Chapter-3-shaped

- `DecompositionStatus` gains **`RESOLVED_CONTEXTUAL_MERGE`**: source
  accounted for, content carried elsewhere, no standalone artifact expected.
- Units gain **`contextualCoverage`**, naming the carrying artifact, the
  policy, the date and the reason. A validator requires it when the status is

  behind it.

  `resolvedAt`.
- `derivedStatusFor()` now lets a recorded decision outrank the derivation.
  The `humanJudgementQuestion` is kept as the record of what was asked.

the same machinery.
