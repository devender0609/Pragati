## Reading a section and teaching from it are now different questions

Checkpoint 19 derived a section's state from the units citing it, so a review or
reference section could never be marked inspected however carefully it had been
read — the only escape was to invent a lesson for it. `OfficialSectionDisposition`
now records a no-unit role (PRACTICE, REVIEW, REFERENCE, ENRICHMENT,
NON_INSTRUCTIONAL, OTHER_EXPLICIT_ROLE), and `sectionInspectionState()` asks for
a verified body, complete evidence, and a disposition **of either kind**.

The guarantee that caught the Chapter 1, 2 and 4 misattributions is kept, in its
corrected form: every section of a decomposed chapter needs **a unit or a
justified no-unit role** — never nothing. A fixture takes a real section, hides
its units, gives it a REVIEW role, and requires FULLY_INSPECTED; under
checkpoint 19 that returned NOT_STARTED.

**No Class 7 section has needed a no-unit disposition yet** — every section read
so far produces at least one unit. The model supports one without inventing a
lesson, which is the point.

## The section denominator is sections

`officialRecordsIndexedOnly = 9` was the count of unread **chapters** sitting in
a field whose denominator is 65 **sections**. Every `officialRecords*` value now
derives from `officialRecordAccounting()`, `officialRecordsNotStarted` is
stored, and a test requires the five section states to sum to 65. The chapter
layer keeps `chapters*` to itself.

## Class 6→7 transition — regenerated, still interim

The audit now reflects the 8 completed chapters and adds two findings:
**proof arrives with triangles** (chapter 7 cannot be authored before chapter
5 — the angle-sum proof depends on it), and **Working with Fractions is not a
repeat of Class 6**: multiplication and division are new, both derived rather
than asserted, and the chapter asks whether a product is larger or smaller than
its factors, which Class 6 never does. The earlier findings stand — parallelism
and the transversal have no Class 6 precursor beyond angle measure, and parity
becomes an argument for impossibility. **No blanket "no prerequisite gap" claim
is made**; seven chapters remain unread.
