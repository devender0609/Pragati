## What changed, and why

**§7.6 is PARTIAL_MATCH, not MULTI_UNIT_COVERAGE.** It represents a section
that yields three units but teaches one of them. MULTI_UNIT_COVERAGE means a
lesson materially teaches several units; letting "same textbook section" earn
that label would have made the category describe the book rather than the
lesson.

**§7.8 remains MULTI_UNIT_COVERAGE**, verified complete: it genuinely teaches
both like- and unlike-denominator arithmetic.

**§7.9 remains PARTIAL_MATCH / VERIFIED_PARTIAL**: history taught, the
unit-fraction decomposition demand not.
