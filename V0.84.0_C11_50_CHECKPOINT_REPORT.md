## Two real defects, found by measurement

### 1. Point labels collided at every viewport

Every label used the same fixed `+7 / -5` offset. That is fine for
scattered points, but a divided segment puts A, P and B on one line a
short way apart, so their labels stacked — **5 collisions at 1280, 834 and
360 alike**, worst `"C (0, 0, 0)"` against `"Q (7/3, 11/3, 5/3)"`
overlapping **50 x 13 px**.

Fixed with a **general declutter pass** in the `coordinate_3d` renderer:
each label takes the first of eight candidate offsets that clears the
labels already placed, with the historical position tried first so
scattered figures are unchanged. **The geometry is untouched** — only the
text moves, and each dot stays exactly where the projection puts it. Not
an FO-10 branch: any figure with crowded points benefits.

### 2. Two figure descriptions were incomplete

The fractional figure never said the division was *internal*; the
constant-z figure stated **neither the ratio nor the internal division**.
A sighted reader gets both from the picture; a screen-reader user got
neither. Both rewritten.
