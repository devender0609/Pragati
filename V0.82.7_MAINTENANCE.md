## What v0.82.6 left half-done

v0.82.6 relabelled the panel "Authoring plan — Chapter 7, Fractions" and
scoped its readiness count, then left the six planning metrics beneath
that heading calling the product-wide `planSummary()`. So the panel
still mixed two scopes: a Chapter 7 heading over product-wide figures
for official records, plannable, deeper structure, authoring items,
design decisions and waivers — and a product-wide headline.

The v0.82.6 test did not notice, because it only checked the heading and
the deprecated readiness call. Its description ("the authoring plan
names its scope") claimed more than it proved.

## The fix

`summarisePlans(plans, scope)` holds the counting logic once.

- `planSummary()` calls it with every plan — **unchanged in meaning and
  output**, and still used by the product-wide Admin views that
  legitimately want the whole picture.
- `planSummaryForChapter(id, label)` calls it with
  `planForChapter(id)` and nothing else.

One summariser means the two scopes cannot drift apart. The panel's
heading, readiness count, headline and every metric now describe the
same chapter.

## The test now proves the claim

The panel does not call `planSummary()`, does call
`planSummaryForChapter(FRACTIONS_CHAPTER_ID, …)`, and the chapter
summary's records, plannable and deeper-structure counts are asserted to
equal what `planForChapter` alone produces. A further assertion shows
the product-wide and chapter figures genuinely differ — which is why the
mixed scope was misleading rather than harmless.
