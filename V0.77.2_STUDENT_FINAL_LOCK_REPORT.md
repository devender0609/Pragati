## §1/§2 — the audience contract

You were right that this was the same error one level deeper. v0.77.1
removed the teacher accordion and the governance footer from the student
lesson and then rendered "WHY STUDENTS DO THIS" and "HOW TO FIX IT"
three times on Worked examples.

Fixed at the record, not at the screenshot. One authored misconception,
three readings, mathematics stored once:

- **Student** — "What can go wrong" / "Try this instead", second person,
  about the work rather than about the child.
- **Teacher** — the authored `why` and `fix`: a diagnosis and an
  instructional response, which is what a teacher opened the page for.
- **Reviewer** — the pattern label alongside those, so a record can be
  identified when it is being checked.

**New copy, flagged.** The student wording is a new optional field on
`CommonMistake`. It restates the authored `example` and `fix` in second
person and adds no mathematical claim, but it is new student-facing text
and should go past a Class 6 teacher with the rest of the FR.02 draft.
Where the field is absent a lesson shows the student *less* rather than
showing them the teacher's copy.

Regression tests assert student routes cannot render the teacher labels;
the capture harness refuses any screenshot containing them.

## §6 — the practice CTA, audited

"Start a practice set" launched the adaptive picker for the same skill
the lesson teaches — the same questions the Try it stage walks through
one at a time with feedback. Two doors to one room, and the hero's was
the worse one: a bare item run with no teaching around it.

The hero now reads **"Go to Try it"** and switches stage. The
cross-skill set is not removed; it lives in Practice. A teacher keeps
the direct assessment launch, because assigning is their job.

## §5 — and a correction about my own evidence

The masthead clearance is `pt-6 sm:pt-8` and is deliberate.

But the "near-collision" I reported in v0.77.1 was **not real**. A
fullPage screenshot renders `position: sticky` elements at their current
viewport offset, so a page captured after clicking a stage button drew
the masthead partway down the image, sitting on the lesson band. The
layout was fine; the evidence was lying, and I spent part of a round
fixing something that was never broken. The harness now scrolls to the
top before every capture.

## §9 — student audience sweep

Normal student surfaces carry none of: `FR.`, `diff.`, `Reteach`,
misconception ids, "why students", "how to fix", teacher, parent,
reviewer, prototype, draft, approval. All are asserted as *forbidden
markers* on all 45 production captures, so a regression fails a capture
rather than needing to be noticed.

Ordinary words were not banned mechanically: "practice", "questions" and
"answer" remain where they are student instructions.

## §8 — the mathematical rendering standard

See-it's architecture is the pattern for future chapters:

> authored semantic visual → capability detection → richer Math Studio
> renderer where supported → authored renderer where not.

The fraction is read **out of** the authored spec, never chosen in the
component. A renderer may improve presentation. It must not invent a
mathematical claim.

## §16 — LOCK DECLARATION

**The Student visual and product architecture is LOCKED.**

No further aesthetic redesign is planned. Future changes to the Student
experience require one of:

- real usability evidence from students or teachers;
- an accessibility defect;
- a functional defect;
- a new mathematics representation requirement;
- a curriculum or content need.

A further visual idea being possible is not a reason to reopen it.
