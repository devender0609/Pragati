## E. Universal set

Every complement example verifies `A ⊆ U` through the model, and a universe
that fails containment is rejected — tested with `{1,2,3}` against
`A = {1,…,6}`, which throws. The lesson gives the same `A` two valid
universes, `{1,…,10}` and `{1,…,6}`, so the complement comes out
`{7,8,9,10}` under one and empty under the other. **A has not moved; what
counts as outside has.**

## F. Venn semantics

Regions are named by expression, never by shading. `shadedNotation` reports
what a figure asserts, and a difference figure shades `A_minus_B` with the
overlap deliberately clear. A negative fixture proves a figure that wrongly
shades the overlap reports different notation from the correct one, and a
complement region with no universe is refused.

Alt text names the universe, both sets and the region, and a test rejects any
alt text under 40 characters or containing a colour word — **meaning may not
depend on colour.** In the browser, 12/12 SVGs are named or hidden.

## G/H/I/J. The operations

Union deduplicates: `{1,…,8}`, 8 elements, not 11 — the concatenation error
is a worked example. The or is inclusive throughout, asserted by test.
An empty intersection is kept distinct from an empty set.

Difference is directional: `A − B = {1,2,3}`, `B − A = {7,8}`, and the lesson
makes the asymmetry the Think deeper question. It needs no universe, which is
what sets up complement.

Both De Morgan laws are **evaluated on both sides** on finite fixtures, not
quoted, and derived from membership conditions rather than symbol-shuffling.

## AC. Next — Trigonometric Functions

Chapter 3, 8 units, all risk C. It tests a real architectural boundary rather
than a hard topic: **Class 10's `specialAngles` is degree-based and
acute-bound and must stay frozen**, because 65 Class 10 lessons depend on its
current behaviour. Radians, the unit circle, quadrant signs and functions over
ℝ each break that assumption directly.

The decision to make there is the one Sets did not force: build a radian-based
function model *beside* `specialAngles`, not as an extension of it. Chapter 1
proved a new general model can be introduced cleanly; Chapter 3 will test
whether two models for neighbouring mathematics can coexist without either
being bent to fit the other.
