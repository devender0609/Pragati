### Two findings that will matter at decomposition

**FO-4 has no home chapter.** Principle of Mathematical Induction does not
appear in the Class XI course structure at all, and the rationalised
14-chapter textbook has no induction chapter. It exists *only* in the
formative-only annexure, with CBSE supplying the NCERT material separately.
Every other formative-only topic maps to a chapter; this one maps to nothing
in `kemh1`.

**FO-12 is foundational to its own summative topic.** Sample space in set
representation is formative-only, while probability of events is summative
core. The foundation is assessed more lightly than what is built on it.

**11 of 14 chapters are mixed-scope** — they carry both summative core and
formative-only material. Only Chapters 6, 10 and 13 are wholly summative.
No chapter may be classified formative-only as a whole.
