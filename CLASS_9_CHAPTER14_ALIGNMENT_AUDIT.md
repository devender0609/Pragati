## Why no solid is drawn

The chapter's own method is flat. Surface area is taught by unfolding, the
cylinder by unrolling, the cone by rotating a triangle, the sphere by winding
thread. A perspective drawing of a cuboid hides three of the six faces whose
areas are being added, so it states the mathematics **less** honestly than the
net does. Choosing 2-D here is a pedagogical judgement that happens to also
avoid an extension, not a limitation worked around.

## Defects found and corrected

- Seven figure points were unlabelled across the cylinder net and the sphere
  figure; the figure validator caught all seven and they are now named.
- Two worked-example steps carried reasoning too thin to explain the step
  ("Substituting the radius"); both were rewritten to say what the substitution
  was into.
- The renderer dropped `shadedRegions[].label`, which the spec has carried since
  checkpoint 32 — so a net could name its faces in data and show none of those
  names on the page. Fixed in the renderer; no schema change.

## What this audit does not do

It does not review the mathematics for a human. All 7 lessons remain
`authored_draft`.
