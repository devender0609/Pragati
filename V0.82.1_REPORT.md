## §3.1 — rewritten, not edited

The lesson was replaced and `contentArtifactVersion` bumped to 2, so a
reviewer comparing versions sees a new artifact rather than a diff.

**Now:** plants of different heights stand in a row, and each position
reports how many of its ADJACENT neighbours are taller — 0, 1 or 2. The
work is decoding, and then deciding which sequences can occur at all.
Two forced facts carry it: an end position has one neighbour and can
never report 2, and the tallest always reports 0.

**Removed entirely** (not moved — no later section teaches them):
numbers needing a unit, "more is not always better", mistakes and
absences, comparing counts from different-sized groups, list position
versus value. All three interactive items went with them.

**The visual waiver is void.** It rested on the wrong reading of the
section. The real mathematics is spatial and relational, so §3.1 now has
two visuals built on the existing `NumberGridSpec` — a row of heights,
and a descending row whose ends both refuse to report 2. No new
representation was needed: a row of numbers where adjacency matters is
exactly what that spec already models.

**Practice** decodes a position, rejects an impossible code, and asks
what the tallest always reports.

**Misconceptions** rewritten to the real errors: counting every taller
object rather than the adjacent ones, counting shorter instead of
taller, giving an end position two neighbours, reading the code as a
height. Two carry a diagnostic signal. `counts_shorter_neighbours`
carries `null` — a reversed count is invisible in one answer and obvious
across a whole row, and we only ever see one.

## §3.3 — rewritten

**Now:** read the two ends, work out what one interval is worth, then
place or read. Three windows: 1000–10,000 in nine intervals, a
ten-wide window around 15,073, and an 86,000–88,000 window in eight
intervals where the step is 250 rather than a round thousand.

**Removed:** constant-versus-doubling steps, widening and shrinking
gaps, the 10-20-60 list-versus-distance hinge. No geometric sequence
appears anywhere in the section.

**`NumberLineSpec` needed no extension.** `min` and `max` are exact
fractions, so a window from 86,000 to 88,000 is expressible with
denominator 1 exactly as a 0-to-1 line is. Non-zero starts, tight
large-number windows and unlabelled ticks were all already supported.

**Misconceptions** rewritten: assuming a line starts at zero, assuming
ticks step by one, reading the picture instead of the scale, placing by
how digits look. Two diagnosable, two not.

## §19 — alignment is now a gate

The audit exposed a hole: a section can populate every structural field
and still teach the wrong mathematics. §3.1 and §3.3 were exactly that
for three releases — `complete_instructional_draft`, and wrong.

`isReviewEligible()` requires completeness **and** an acceptable
alignment status, and **fails closed**: a section with no alignment
record is not eligible, because an unaudited section is not an aligned
one.
