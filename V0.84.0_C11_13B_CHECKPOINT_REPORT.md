## C. The guard is structural

It scans the authored **source** for predicates interpolated with no formatter
between them and the reader, and covers a leading `!` — which is how one leak
survived the first pass.

A rendered-prose scan cannot do this job. It cannot distinguish a leaked
boolean from the ordinary English of *"and that remains true"*, a sentence the
angle-between-lines lesson is entitled to contain. The loose pattern ` true\.`
was narrowed to `:\s*(?:true|false|null|undefined|NaN|Infinity)\b` and the
source scan carries the guarantee. **That is precision, not weakening:** the
structural check is strictly stronger and the pattern it replaced was
producing a false positive on correct mathematics.

The browser harness made the same mistake once and was corrected the same way.
