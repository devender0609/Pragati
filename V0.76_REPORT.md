# Pragati v0.76.0 — Product redesign, first pass

**Status: the three north-star surfaces changed. The teacher product did not.**

This release answers the instruction that v0.69–v0.75 styling was not
visually accepted: too white, too grey, too card-heavy, too flat, too
sparse on desktop, too close to internal school software. §1 said to fix
three flagship surfaces before touching the rest of the app. That is
what happened, and the parts that did not get touched are named in §6
below rather than implied.

## 2. Two defects found by reading the code, not the screenshots

**The display typeface was never loaded.** `tailwind.config.js` has
declared `font-display: 'Bricolage Grotesque'` since v0.69.
`index.html` only ever loaded Inter. Every `font-display` class in the
product — every heading, every chapter title, every number — has
silently rendered as Inter for six releases. Six releases of work on
"expressive headings" was one typeface wearing a different class name.
No screenshot review could have caught this, because the fallback is a
good typeface.

**The teacher workspace could not use its own width.** `main` is capped
at `max-w-6xl` (1152px) while `LAYOUT.workspace`, added in v0.75,
declares `max-w-[90rem]`. The wider workspace was unreachable. `main` is
now `max-w-[84rem]`.

## 3. What actually changed, and why

### The ground, not the accents

The diagnosis in the brief was structurally right. Every screen was
slate-50 behind white cards with hairline rings, and no accent survives
a ground that occupies 80% of the pixels. Two new colour families —
`ink`, a deep indigo-navy for large fields, and `paper`, a warm
low-chroma page — carry **no meaning at all**. That is the point: the
ten semantic ramps kept their exact jobs and stopped having to also
perform "this screen is interesting".

### A pattern language: the pulli lattice

A kolam is drawn on a grid of dots set out on a threshold, and the
figure is defined entirely by how one line travels around them. It is
Indian, it is geometric, it is the oldest drawing-on-a-lattice practice
there is, and it is what mathematics looks like before anyone writes it
down.

Every coloured field in Pragati now carries that lattice, and all ten
chapter illustrations are constructed on it. This is what makes §3's
actual test pass: a screenshot with the wordmark removed still reads as
Pragati, because the geometry sits on visible dots.

### Ten large chapter drawings

`ChapterMotif` (24×24 icons) is kept where an icon is the right answer.
`ChapterArtwork` is new and separate, composed at 240×180 with real
internal structure, in three tones. Fractions shows three
representations of the same number side by side — a partitioned bar, a
partitioned circle, and the same length marked on a number line.
Constructions is drawn mid-step with the compass arcs left visible,
because in that chapter the arcs are the work. Perimeter and Area is one
rectangle carrying both ideas at once, since telling those apart is the
chapter's entire difficulty.

Upcoming chapters get the **same drawing** at a quieter tone. §6 asked
for beautifully muted rather than grey disabled boxes, and drawing them
properly is the only honest way to do that.

### Typography

- **Anek Latin** for display. Drawn by Ek Type (Mumbai) as one
  superfamily across nine Indian scripts, so the Latin and Devanagari
  were designed by the same hand for the same page.
- **Anek Devanagari** for प्रगति, so the wordmark's two scripts share a
  skeleton.
- **Inter** kept for body, UI and data. A product a child reads for ten
  minutes should not experiment there.

Both new families are SIL OFL and served from Google Fonts, so there is
no production licensing question.

Two rules a general type scale cannot express were added: figures are
tabular so a column of answers does not wobble, and `Frac` stacks
numerator over denominator with a real rule — `3/5` in running text is a
division sign a Class 6 student has not met yet. It is announced to a
screen reader as "three fifths".

### The mark

v0.69 replaced a letter in a square with three ascending bars and a
chevron. Better than a letter, still a generic growth glyph that would
belong to any analytics product of the last decade. The mark is now one
continuous line rising through a nine-dot lattice: a single unbroken
path that rises, drawn on the grid the chapter artwork is drawn on.

### Composition

`Band`, `Zone`, `Lede`, `Frac`, `Fact` — none of which draws a border.
§10's rule is that almost nothing needs to be a bordered rounded
rectangle: a heading with space under it groups content, a tint groups
content, a coloured field groups content, and a border is the weakest of
the four.

`Lede` has **no eyebrow slot**, deliberately. v0.75 set an uppercase
tracked label above nearly every section in the product — "IN
FRACTIONS", "YOUR CHAPTERS", "COMING SOON", "PRACTICE YOU CAN DO NOW" —
and the cumulative effect is a screen that shouts six small labels
before it says anything.

## 4. Three corrections made mid-flight

These are recorded because the iterations that produced them are the
method §1 asked for, and because two of them were mistakes.

**The first pass reproduced the bug it was fixing.** My first Home
capture had a ~500px void beside the parts list whenever a student had
no recorded activity — the exact v0.75 failure this release exists to
remove. It is now a two-column grid only when the second column has
something real in it, and a first-run student gets an honest orientation
strip describing what a part of a chapter actually contains.

**A violet gradient across 990×320 is not an area of colour, it is a
wash.** My first chapter hero ran the chapter hue as a linear gradient
across the whole field, and for Fractions — whose hue is violet — that
reproduced "one violet rectangle", bigger. The field is now ink, with
the chapter hue arriving as light: a broad glow where the type sits and
a tighter one behind the artwork.

**I made upcoming chapters tappable and the suite stopped me.** I
reasoned that muted should not mean unreachable, since there is a real
page behind an upcoming chapter. Two tests disagreed — v0.63 §11 and
v0.69 §34 both pin that unavailable chapters are **non-interactive**,
because a chapter that accepts a tap and then has nothing in it was the
original defect. The tests were right and I reverted. `ChapterPlate`
carries that reasoning in a comment so the next person does not
re-derive it.

### One contract changed on purpose

`student_coming_soon` required the literal string "Coming soon". The
product now says "Being written", which is more honest — it says what is
happening rather than making a marketing promise. The contract was
updated **with its reasoning written into the file**, and it still
enforces the same three obligations: the first chapter is visible, the
last chapter is visible, and unreadiness is stated plainly.

Two harness targets were also repointed, both because a label moved
rather than because a capability was lost: the featured chapter's action
on Learn moved from a tile labelled "Chapter 7" into the hero band,
whose action is "Open this chapter". I briefly renamed that button to
"Open Fractions" to satisfy the harness, then reverted it — two v0.69
truthfulness tests pin the label, and bending product copy to please a
screenshot script is the wrong trade.

## 6. What this release did not do

**Teacher Overview and Teacher Assign are not redesigned.** They inherit
the new ground, the new mark, the wider container and the sentence-case
mode toggle, and nothing else. Their composition is v0.75's: a violet
card and three white cards. Teacher Assign still sits outside
`TeacherShell` entirely. §12 and §13 — premium SaaS density, intentional
data visualisation, a teacher theme distinct from the student one — are
untouched.

This was the instruction, not an oversight: §1 says do not redesign the
app until the three flagship surfaces reach the target. But the wider
container makes the teacher screens *emptier* than they were, so v0.76
is a small step backwards on teacher desktop until §12 is done.

**Also not done:** motion (§9) beyond a reduced-motion floor; the
in-lesson mathematical visuals, which are still flat blue blocks and do
not yet speak the lattice language; the first-run marketing onboarding,
which is the first screen a new visitor sees and is still entirely
v0.75; Number Play authoring; the admin surfaces.

Teacher, in one pass, to §12 and §13 — starting with Assign, which is
the worst screen in the product and still sits outside the shell. The
student north stars are now a standard the teacher work can be measured
against, which is the thing v0.76 was actually for.

review packages.** They have been ready for a release and a half, and no
amount of further design work determines whether the 16-question
instrument is the right length. A real reviewer answering one does.
