## The release in one paragraph

v0.73 shipped a plan that said **"89 official records need work · 483 authoring
items · 8 blocked on people, not engineering."** Every one of those three
numbers was wrong, in a way that read as precision. This release does not add a
governance layer on top of them; it replaces the model that produced them and
adds standing guards so the same class of error fails the suite rather than
shipping in a headline.

# PLANNING

### 1. Was the universal Fractions-shape assumption removed?

Yes. `targetSectionShape()` is gone.

It returned the **median of nine Class 6 Fractions sections** — 6 explanation
paragraphs, 3 worked examples, 3 guided, 5 independent, 2 visuals, 2
interactive — and applied it to every backlog record, including Class 11
Calculus units.

Replaced by `productionStage.ts`, which resolves a standard per stage:

| Stage | Standard |
|---|---|
| Primary (early) · 1–2 | `production_standard_pending` |
| Primary · 3–5 | `production_standard_pending` |
| **Middle · 6–8** | **audited standard** (Chapter 7, section grain) |
| Secondary · 9–10 | `production_standard_pending` |
| Senior secondary · 11–12 | `production_standard_pending` |

The obvious fix — writing five shapes — would have replaced one unevidenced
number with five. Pragati has audited exactly one body of content, so there is
exactly one standard. Four stages returning "pending" is not a gap in the file;
it is the true state of the evidence.

The measurement survives, renamed `observedMiddleStageFractionsShape()`. **The
name was the defect**: a reader of the planner saw `targetSectionShape` and
reasonably concluded it was Pragati's target.

`assertNoShapeLeakage()` is a standing guard and returns empty.

### 2. How does the planner distinguish grade/stage?

`PRODUCTION_STAGE_FOR_GRADE` maps all twelve grades to five production bands.
These are **deliberately separate** from `ncfStages.ts`, which models NCF-SE
*competency* stages and is two-valued because that is how the framework numbers
its curricular goals. A test asserts the two models never disagree about a
grade where both are defined.

### 3. How does it distinguish unit/chapter/section/topic?

`curriculumGrain.ts`, reading registry fields that already existed —
`topLevel`, `level`, `chaptersEstablished`, `topicsKnown`. The planner simply
never read them.

- **65** records are `official_section` (all Class 6) → plannable
- **24** records are `official_unit` (Classes 9–12) → **not** plannable

Class 10 Unit II is "Algebra": 20 marks, four topics, each a chapter's worth of
teaching. v0.73 planned it at 6 explanation paragraphs and 3 worked examples,
identically to §7.4. Those records now return
`requires_deeper_curriculum_structure` and a single
`verify_official_structure` work item naming the evidence required.

### 4. Was the 483-item count wrong?

**Yes — in both directions at once.**

| | v0.73 | v0.74 |
|---|---|---|
| Records | 89 | 89 |
| Plannable | *(not distinguished)* | 65 |
| Requires deeper structure | *(not distinguished)* | 24 |
| Counted authoring items | **483** | **339** |
| Design decisions | *(silently omitted)* | **168** |
| Waived with reason | *(silently dropped)* | **3** |
| Review packages to build | *(not modelled)* | **7** |

Two independent errors:

- **144 items were planned at the wrong grain** (24 unit records × 6 items).
- **Three whole components produced zero items.** The target shape named
  `reasoningTasks`, `interactivePractice` and `documentedMisconceptions`; the
  unauthored branch emitted work for none of them across all 80 unauthored
  records. 483 undercounted its own specification while overcounting records it
  should not have planned.

483 is not a number to correct. It is a model to replace.

### 5. What is the corrected known workload?

**339 determined authoring items + 168 design decisions**, across 65 plannable
records, all Class 6. Reported separately because they are not the same kind of
thing and summing them would rebuild the defect.

Plus **24 records** needing curriculum structure verification, and **7 review
packages** needing engineering.

### 6. Which requirements are undetermined pending pedagogical design?

Three, for each of the 56 unauthored sections: **semantic visual**,
**interactive practice item**, **documented misconception**.

These were chosen from evidence rather than taste: they are exactly the three
that Chapter 7 *actually waived* for §7.9, with written reasons. A component
that has never needed a waiver in nine audited sections is treated as required.

Applicability is now three-valued — `required`, `not_required_with_reason`,
`undetermined_requires_design_review` — and a test asserts all three appear.
An unknown produces a **design task**, never a silent omission.

# FUNCTIONALITY

### 7. Is every Student top-level tab now independently verified?

Yes. `student_practice` had **never** been captured or contracted despite being
in the bottom nav since v0.49. It now has a route contract, a capture at
390/768/1440, and 14 DOM tests.

### 8. Does Practice work end-to-end?

Yes — and auditing it found a real defect.

Class 6 Practice listed **"Decimals", "Ratio & Proportion" and "Algebra
Basics"** beside "Fractions" and "Symmetry", as plain chapter titles. Those
three have **no chapter in the current Ganita Prakash Grade 6 book**.
`legacyDisposition.ts` records the evidence and `isClass6Core()` already
withheld them from Learn. **Practice never asked.**

A Class 6 student was shown "Decimals" as though it were part of their
textbook. Nothing was removed — the questions are real and working — but
displaced material is now separated under "Extra practice" and labelled, as it
already was on the Fractions landing. The tab also gained the v0.72 §13
qualifier: *"not the chapter lessons themselves."*

### 9. Is Teacher Assess independently verified?

Yes, and it is the most serious finding of the release. See Q10.

Assess is **entirely the formal Growth path** — there is no ordinary
instructional-check surface behind it. That is defensible (Growth is frozen and
the panel refuses honestly) but it must be *labelled*. The `teacher_assess`
contract now requires "Pragati Growth" and "Separate from everyday practice",
and forbids `RIT`, `percentile`, `mastery`.

### 10. What visible dead controls were found?

**Two entire teacher workflows were unreachable on a phone.**

v0.71 §17 cut the phone nav from six tabs to four and left this comment:

> *Nothing is removed — Assess and Insights are reachable from the Overview and
> from the desktop header.*

It was false. `TeacherOverviewBody` accepted only `onOpenAssign` and
`onOpenClasses`; the header nav is `hidden` below `md`. Measured at 390, the
Assess button reported **`width 0, height 0`**.

Assess and Insights were unreachable on a phone for **three releases, behind a
comment asserting they were not.** Neither was ever captured or contracted, so
nothing failed.

Fixed: Overview carries real entry points below `md`, and the false comment is
corrected in place rather than deleted. Verified at 390, where the added
control is the only visible route.

`tools/interactionMatrix.mjs` now clicks every visible primary CTA at all three
widths: **57 probes, 0 failures.**

> **Method note.** Its first run reported 27 failures. All were the harness:
> it clicked the *first* text match, which was the hidden desktop nav. It now
> selects on **visibility** — a control a person cannot see is not a control
> they can use. That change is what exposed the genuine defect above.

### 20. What is the recommended Class 6 production order?

| # | Chapter | Sections left | Ready? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Number Play | 12 | ready |
| 2 | The Other Side of Zero | 5 | ready |
| 3 | Prime Time | 6 | ready |

arc.

considerations, and `assertChapterSpecificPedagogy()` fails if one inherits the
Fractions shape without comment. Constructions is ranked **last** with the
reason stated — a multiple-choice question about a construction assesses recall
of a procedure the student never performed, which is the §7.9 failure mode at
chapter scale.
