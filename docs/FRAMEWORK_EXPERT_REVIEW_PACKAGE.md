# Pragati Mathematics Framework — Expert Review Package

**For review by Indian Mathematics educators.**
**Status: AWAITING REVIEW. Not approved.**

Prepared 2026-08-19. This package asks for your judgement on decisions
that software cannot settle. Please disagree freely — a recorded
disagreement is more useful to us than a comfortable consensus.

Estimated time: 60–90 minutes.

---

## 1. What Pragati Growth is meant to be

An interim Mathematics assessment for Indian schools, given a few times
a year, intended eventually to show a teacher where each student is
across Mathematics domains and how that changes over time.

**What it is today:** a set of questions that records which competencies
were sampled and how the student responded. It has no calibrated items,
no scale, no norms, and reports no score. Everything below is
preparation for measurement, not measurement.

**We are asking you to review the framework before items are written**,
because rewriting items after a framework change is the expensive part.

## 4. Decisions we need you to rule on

### DECISION 1 — Should "Fractions & Rational Number Reasoning" be its own reported domain?

**We propose: yes, as an explicit Pragati design choice.**

*For:* rational-number reasoning has a long developmental progression
and is a persistent difficulty; teachers can act on it directly.

*Against:* **NCF-SE does not separate it.** CG-1 covers natural, whole,
integer, rational, irrational and real numbers in one goal. A domain
that does not exist in the curriculum may confuse teachers, and it
takes items away from Number.

**We have labelled this a Pragati measurement-design distinction, not
curriculum alignment.** Is that honest labelling sufficient, or should
it be folded into Number Sense?

### DECISION 2 — How should Computational Thinking be treated?

**We propose: an item-level process tag, NOT a reported domain.**

*Evidence:* CBSE states CT is integrated "across school subjects,
including beyond Mathematics, **as a cross-cutting theme**"; its
learning standards are "foundational capacities that cut across
disciplines"; and it is assessed via projects, reflective journals and
teacher observation.

*For:* reporting a CT score inside a Mathematics test would attribute a
cross-subject capability to one subject, and a 35-item multiple-choice
form cannot measure what CBSE assesses through projects.

*Against:* CT is a major official emphasis — 50 hours a year at Classes
3–5 and 100 at 6–8, with resource books aligned chapter-by-chapter to
the Mathematics textbook. Schools may reasonably expect to see it.

☐ Item tag only ☐ Reported domain ☐ Separate CT instrument ☐ Other: ______

---

### DECISION 3 — Should Patterns be a domain?

**We propose: no** — merge into Algebraic Thinking, and tag pattern
recognition as a CT process.

*For:* Pattern Recognition is named as a CT element; sequences map to
algebra and modelling at secondary; a 5% domain yields ~2 items.

*Against:* pattern work is central at the foundational and preparatory
stages, and may deserve a stage-specific domain for younger children.

☐ Merge into Algebra ☐ Keep as a domain ☐ Stage-specific ☐ Other: ______

### DECISION 4 — Should Data and Probability be one domain?

**We propose: yes, one domain.** NCF-SE treats them as one goal (CG-6,
with C-6.1 and C-6.2 as siblings), and separately they would be ~3 and
~2 items.

### DECISION 5 — Is the Rational Number progression ordering correct?

Our proposed order:

equal partitioning → unit fractions → representing fractions →
equivalence → comparing/ordering → adding with unlike denominators →
decimals → multiplying/dividing → ratio → proportion → percentage →
algebraic proportionality

*Questions:*
- Is any step out of order?
- Is any necessary step missing?
- Should decimals come **before** unlike-denominator addition?
- Is placing decimals at Class 7 correct? Pragati's legacy content
  treated them as Class 6, but the current textbook appears not to.

## 7. Governance

- **Two independent qualified reviewers are required** before the pilot
  framework is frozen.
- If reviewers disagree, **the disagreement is recorded and the
  framework stays unfrozen.** It is not resolved by majority or by us
  picking the more convenient answer.
- Reviewer identities are never fabricated. Until real reviewers
  complete this document, the framework status stays `draft_internal`
  and no secure items are authored.
